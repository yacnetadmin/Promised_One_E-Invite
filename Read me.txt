CALENDAR AND DIRECTIONS
Cards automatically disappear at their calendar end time: December 5 at 4 PM and 9 PM Eastern, and December 12 at 5 PM Eastern.
Remaining performance/location counts, desktop columns, and shared invitation text update too. An open calendar dialog closes if its performance expires.
After the final performance, the schedule shows a Christmas thank-you message and hides calendar, directions, childcare, sharing, and event navigation actions.
Checks run on page load, every second while the page is running, and when returning to the page. Timing uses the visitor's device clock and UTC end times, independent of their timezone.
With JavaScript disabled, the original schedule remains visible with an explanatory note. Social/message previews and the artwork do not automatically change.
A card's calendar link opens only the Google Calendar / Apple / Outlook options for that specific performance, with its date, time, and location displayed. There is no repeat performance question.
Google Calendar links and Apple / Outlook .ics files use the matching location and UTC time.
On iPhone, iPad, and Mac, Add to Apple Calendar is the first, prominent option. It opens the event's HTTPS .ics file in the same tab, without forcing a download or a new browser tab. Actual import behavior depends on the device.
Google Calendar is labeled (web), with a browser/sign-in explanation. Both options remain available; there is no forced app launch or calendar subscription.
With JavaScript disabled, cards link directly to their .ics files, directions open Google Maps, and bottom links scroll to performances or sharing instructions.
Directions opens the card's location directly in Apple Maps on Apple devices and Google Maps elsewhere, with no location question.
Existing #calendar and #directions links now scroll to the performance cards when JavaScript is enabled.
App launch and calendar imports depend on browser, installed apps, and device settings.

SHARING
Share invitation opens the device share sheet where supported. Otherwise it exposes a copyable public link.
If clipboard access fails, the selected link can be copied manually. No claim of success is shown on failure or cancellation.
Social preview metadata uses the public GitHub Pages address. If hosting changes, update the canonical, Open Graph, and fallback input URLs in index.html, plus the matching test.
Text-message preview display and caching are controlled by the recipient's messaging app.
